# Product

## Platform

ios

## Product Purpose

CVee organizes work history and target jobs into tailored, editable resumes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing Tasks, Saved Jobs, Resume Wizard, Resumes, and Profile workflows, confirmed by the user. SwiftUI and SwiftData provide native UI and local persistence. Generation, import analysis, and enhancement remain on-device with Foundation Models availability guards. Resume facts come only from user input. Preserve PDF/RTF export and accessibility.

Resume reports provide transparent, local phrase coverage against a user-confirmed job checklist, related work-library evidence, and document-health findings from the editable document and exported PDF. Coverage is not an ATS score and does not verify proficiency, eligibility, or hiring outcomes. Findings never modify resume text or block saving/exporting. Apple Intelligence suggestions are optional and validated against their supplied text.

## Brand Commitments

Apply the supplied Asana-inspired coral-and-charcoal design reference to the existing CVee workflows.
